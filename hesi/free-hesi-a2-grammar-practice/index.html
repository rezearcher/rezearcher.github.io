What the HESI A2 Grammar section tests

The Grammar section covers parts of speech, subject–verb agreement, correct pronoun use, common usage errors, punctuation, and clear sentence structure. Free Grammar practice questions

1. Which sentence demonstrates correct subject-verb agreement?

A. The box of surgical gloves are on the supply cart.B. The nurse, along with two students, is reviewing the chart.C. Each of the patients are scheduled for a morning exam.D. Neither the doctor nor the nurses was available.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The subject 'nurse' is singular, and the phrase 'along with two students' does not change it, so the singular verb 'is' is correct.

2. Choose the sentence that uses the correct pronoun.

A. The charge nurse assigned Maria and I to the pediatric unit.B. Between you and I, the shift was exhausting.C. The nurse gave the instructions to him and me.D. Her and the technician reviewed the lab results.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Both 'him' and 'me' are objects of the preposition 'to,' so the objective case is required.
