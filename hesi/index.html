Reading Comprehension

3. Thirst often becomes a less reliable signal as people age. Older adults also tend to have less total body water than younger adults, and some take medications that increase fluid loss. As a result, they can become dehydrated before they feel the need to drink. For this reason, many caregivers offer fluids at regular intervals instead of waiting for a request. What is the main idea of this passage?

A. Most medications taken by older adults cause severe dehydration.B. Caregivers often fail to notice when older adults are thirsty.C. Age-related changes increase the risk of dehydration, so fluids should be offered proactively.D. Older adults need more total fluid each day than younger adults.

Answer: C. The passage describes several reasons older adults are vulnerable to dehydration and concludes that caregivers should offer fluids before they are requested.

4. Bone is living tissue that is constantly being rebuilt. Cells called osteoclasts dissolve small areas of old bone, and cells called osteoblasts then lay down new material in those spaces. During childhood, new bone forms faster than old bone is removed. Later in adulthood, this balance gradually shifts, and bone density may decline. According to the passage, what happens after osteoclasts dissolve small areas of old bone?

A. Osteoblasts lay down new material in those spaces.B. Bone density immediately begins to decline.C. Osteoclasts convert into osteoblasts.D. The empty spaces remain until adulthood.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The passage directly states that osteoblasts lay down new material in the spaces left by osteoclasts.

A. The medication should be taken with foodB. The medication should not be used because it may cause harmC. The medication must be given at a higher doseD. The medication is only available by prescription
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. A contraindication is a condition or factor that makes a particular treatment or drug inadvisable because it could be harmful.

6. Which word best completes the sentence? The eye doctor placed drops in the patient's eyes to ______ the pupils before the examination.

A. constrictB. dilateC. occludeD. lubricate
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. To dilate means to widen or enlarge, which is the purpose of mydriatic drops used before an eye exam.

Grammar

7. Which sentence demonstrates correct subject-verb agreement?

A. The box of surgical gloves are on the supply cart.B. The nurse, along with two students, is reviewing the chart.C. Each of the patients are scheduled for a morning exam.D. Neither the doctor nor the nurses was available.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The subject 'nurse' is singular, and the phrase 'along with two students' does not change it, so the singular verb 'is' is correct.

8. Choose the sentence that uses the correct pronoun.

A. The charge nurse assigned Maria and I to the pediatric unit.B. Between you and I, the shift was exhausting.C. The nurse gave the instructions to him and me.D. Her and the technician reviewed the lab results.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Both 'him' and 'me' are objects of the preposition 'to,' so the objective case is required.

Biology

9. Which organelle is the primary site of ATP production through aerobic respiration in eukaryotic cells?

A. Golgi apparatusB. MitochondrionC. LysosomeD. Rough endoplasmic reticulum
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Mitochondria carry out the citric acid cycle and oxidative phosphorylation, generating most of the cell's ATP.

10. A human somatic cell with 46 chromosomes undergoes mitosis. How many chromosomes will each daughter cell contain?

A. 23B. 46C. 92D. 69
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Mitosis produces two genetically identical diploid daughter cells, each with the same chromosome number as the parent (46).

Answer: B. Proximal means nearer to the point of attachment to the trunk, so a site proximal to the wrist lies closer to the elbow.

14. Which chamber of the heart pumps oxygenated blood into the systemic circulation?
