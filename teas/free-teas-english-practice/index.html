What the ATI TEAS English & Language Usage section tests

English & Language Usage checks the conventions of standard written English: grammar and parts of speech, subject-verb and pronoun agreement, punctuation, sentence structure, spelling, and choosing the precise word for a context. Free English & Language Usage practice questions

1. Choose the sentence with correct subject-verb agreement.

A. The list of supplies are on the counter.B. The list of supplies were on the counter.C. The list of supplies is on the counter.D. The list of supplies have been on the counter.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The subject is 'list' (singular); 'of supplies' is a prepositional phrase that does not change the verb. The singular verb 'is' agrees with 'list.'

2. Which sentence is punctuated correctly?

A. After the exam we celebrated, with pizza and music.B. After the exam we celebrated with pizza, and music.C. After, the exam we celebrated with pizza and music.D. After the exam, we celebrated with pizza and music.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. A comma follows the introductory phrase 'After the exam,' and no other comma is needed. The other options misplace the comma.

3. Choose the sentence that uses the correct pronoun.

A. The instructor gave the assignment to Jordan and me.B. The instructor gave the assignment to Jordan and I.C. The instructor gave the assignment to I and Jordan.D. The instructor gave the assignment to myself and Jordan.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. As the object of the preposition 'to,' the objective pronoun 'me' is correct. Testing each pronoun alone ('to me') confirms it.

4. Choose the sentence that uses the correct word.

A. The new policy will effect every department.B. The new policy will affect every department.C. The new policy will have an affect on every department.D. The new policy will effected every department.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 'Affect' is usually the verb meaning to influence; 'effect' is usually the noun meaning a result. The policy will 'affect' (influence) the departments.

5. Which sentence is capitalized correctly?

A. We visited the Pacific ocean last Summer.B. We visited the pacific Ocean last summer.C. We visited the Pacific Ocean last summer.D. we visited the Pacific Ocean last Summer.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 'Pacific Ocean' is a proper noun and is capitalized; the season 'summer' is not capitalized unless it begins a sentence.
