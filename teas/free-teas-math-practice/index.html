1. Evaluate: 24 - 6 × (8 - 5) + 2

Answer: C. Using order of operations: 8 - 5 = 3, then 6 × 3 = 18, then 24 - 18 + 2 = 8.

2. A patient is to receive 30% of a 500 mL bag over the first hour. How many milliliters is that?

Answer: D. 30% of 500 = 0.30 × 500 = 150 mL.

3. What is 3/4 + 1/6, expressed as a single fraction in lowest terms?

Answer: A. Use a common denominator of 12: 3/4 = 9/12 and 1/6 = 2/12, so 9/12 + 2/12 = 11/12, already in lowest terms.

4. If 4 ounces of solution contain 12 milligrams of medication, how many milligrams are in 10 ounces at the same concentration?

Answer: B. Set up a proportion: 12/4 = x/10, so x = 10 × (12/4) = 10 × 3 = 30 mg.
