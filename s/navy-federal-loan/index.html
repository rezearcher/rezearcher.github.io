Navy Federal Unauthorized Personal Loan Settlement: who may qualify and how to file a claim (deadline 2026-11-29)

File your claim by 2026-11-29

Eligible members are NFCU members who were given a personal loan between January 1, 2023 and January 31, 2025 that was applied for from a device NFCU did not recognize. The loan must also have been paid out into the member's account, disputed by the member as fraud, and denied by NFCU in whole or in part, with the funds not fully recovered.
