Hyundai and Kia Airbag Control Unit Settlement (ZF-TRW): who may qualify and how to file a claim (deadline 2027-04-08)

File your claim by 2027-04-08

Anyone, whether an individual or an organization, that owned or leased an eligible Hyundai or Kia vehicle as of April 14, 2025, or had owned or leased one earlier, is part of the settlement class, provided the vehicle was first sold or leased in the U.S. or its territories. Vehicle eligibility is decided by the VIN, which can be entered in a lookup tool to see whether a given car is covered. Owners of vehicles that were never recalled can still file for the residual payment, and no out-of-pocket expenses are required to do so.
