Non-Bank ATM Surcharge Settlement: who may qualify and how to file a claim (deadline 2027-02-10)

File your claim by 2027-02-10

Anyone in the U.S. who paid an access fee for a cash withdrawal at an independent ATM between October 24, 2007 and August 14, 2026, and was not fully reimbursed by their bank, is part of the Nationwide Class. People who were in California, Illinois, Massachusetts, or Michigan when charged such a fee in that period may also fall into a statewide class. Qualifying transactions are withdrawals of cash from a deposit account with an ATM or pin-debit card at an independent machine in the U.S. or its territories; credit card transactions, cash advances and prepaid card transactions are not covered.
