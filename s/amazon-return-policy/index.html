Amazon Return Policy Settlement: who may qualify and how to file a claim (deadline 2026-12-01)

File your claim by 2026-12-01

People may be covered if they started a return or asked for a refund on a physical product sold through Amazon.com and received in the U.S. between September 5, 2017 and February 12, 2026, and then got no refund, a late or wrong refund, or a refund followed by a wrong charge. Some members are paid automatically and don't need to file. Others must file a claim form to be paid. The notice's settlement documents say which group is which.
